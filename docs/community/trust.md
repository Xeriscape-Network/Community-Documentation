# Trust Levels
We believe that a great community is built by the people who show up, hang out, and contribute every day. To celebrate our most reliable members, we’ve implemented a Member Trust System. As you spend time with us and build your reputation, you will automatically progress through various Trust Levels that unlock exclusive perks and additional server permissions!

## Why Trust Levels?
To keep our community safe and high-quality, we use Trust Levels to verify our active members. Rather than outright denying permissions to everyone or granting them all at once, this system allows us to provide specific permissions to members who have proven themselves to be trustworthy over time. By working toward specific thresholds, you demonstrate your commitment to the community, ensuring that "power tools" like image uploads and link sharing are reserved for those who are truly part of the family.

## Earning Experience
You build trust naturally by accumulating Experience (XP) through your activity in the server. This experience acts as the "currency" of your progression; as you reach specific XP milestones, your Trust Level will increase. There are two main ways to earn experience:

- **Text Channels:** Participate in conversations within our various text channels. Whether you're helping someone out or just sharing a story, your contributions build your trust.
- **Voice Channels:** Hanging out in a Voice Chat (VC) also builds trust over time, with experience gained simply by joining the voice channel and participating in shared activities like screen sharing or streaming.

### Anti-Spam Measures
We want building trust to be a natural part of your experience in our Discord server, not a chore. To keep things fair for everyone, we have several systems in place to ensure engagement remains genuine. Please be aware that attempting to "XP Farm" or bypass our limits will result in moderation action, which may include a reset of your Trust Level or being removed from the community entirely.

- **Natural Conversation:** Trust is built through consistent activity and genuine participation in conversations. Sending 50 messages within a 10-second period won't build trust any faster than having a normal, thoughtful conversation; The system is designed to reward real engagement rather than message volume.
- **Quality over Quantity:** Spamming random characters, emojis, or "gibberish" to try and game the system is easily detected.
- **Anti-Idling:** Our system can distinguish between active participation and "ghosting" in channels. You must be actively participating in a voice chat channel to earn experience; simply sitting in a channel alone or idling while muted will not grant progress. Trust is built through genuine interaction with other active members in the channel.

Essentially, just be yourself, participate in the community, and you'll hit those milestones in no time!

## Paragon & Prestige
Paragon represents the pinnacle of community trust. Reaching Level 11 is our prestige milestone, signifying that you are a cornerstone of the community. While your level may continue to rise beyond this point as a reflection of your long-term dedication and activity, all functional permissions and rewards are fully unlocked at Paragon.

**Any levels gained afterward are purely for status and bragging rights.**

## Checking Your Progress
If you are curious about your current Member Trust status, simply head over to the [**#terminal**](https://discord.com/channels/555868544244514836/1059392260095094864) channel and run the `/profile` command. This will display your current standing and a progress bar showing exactly how close you are to reaching the next level!
