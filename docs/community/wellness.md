# Member Wellness

At Xeriscape, we are committed to fostering a supportive and healthy environment for all members. Your well-being, safety, and health are fundamental to the strength of our community.

This guide provides information and resources to help you or a friend navigate mental and emotional health challenges by connecting with individuals and organizations specifically trained to offer professional assistance and care.

## Staff Support
The Xeriscape staff team is here to support the community in many ways, but we are not trained mental health professionals, counselors, or emergency responders.

- **Our Role:** We are here to listen to general concerns and provide the resources listed below.
- **What We Cannot Provide:** One-on-one therapy, professional counseling, or crisis intervention.
- **Concerned for a Member?** If you are worried about a friend or fellow community member, please bring it to staff's attention. We can connect the member with these resources.

## Seeking Professional Support
If you are feeling overwhelmed, depressed, suicidal, or facing other emotional health challenges, please know that you are not alone. Reaching out is a sign of immense strength. The resources below can provide immediate, continuous, and professional care.

!!! warning "For Immediate Risk"
	If you or another person is in immediate danger or facing a life-threatening emergency, stop reading and contact your local emergency services (e.g., 911 in North America) immediately.

## A Community Safe Space
We encourage open communication, but for the safety and well-being of all members, especially those who may be distressed or lack formal training, we ask that conversations regarding acute mental health crises (such as suicidal ideation, self-harm, or severe trauma) be channeled to the individuals listed below.

We recommend avoiding public discussion of these topics within the Xeriscape community channels. This policy is in place to:

1. **Protect Members:** Prevent undue distress or worry about community members and staff who are not equipped to handle a crisis situation.
2. **Ensure Effective Care:** Ensure that those needing help are directed immediately to trained, professional responders who can provide life-saving assistance.

!!! info "Take a Break When Needed"
	We encourage you to prioritize your health first. Please step away as long as you need as your health comes before any online commitment. We'll be here, ready to welcome you back whenever you feel ready to return.

## Direct Support Contacts
We encourage any community member facing difficulties to reach out to someone they trust who can provide support on a continuous and regular basis:

- **Immediate Family:** Parents, siblings, or guardians. 
- **Extended Network:** Grandparents, aunts, uncles, or trusted family friends. 
- **Academic Contacts:** Teachers, school counselors, or trusted administrative staff at educational institutions. 
- **Spiritual/Community Leaders:** Religious figures or local community group leaders. 
- **Trained Professionals:**
    - Counselors, psychologists, or therapists. 
    - A doctor or local General Practitioner (GP).

## Hotlines and Resources
If you do not feel comfortable with the above recommendations, these extended resources offer free, confidential, and professional support 24/7.

### North America

| Service                       | Contact Type                                                   | Availability            |
|-------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline | Call or Text 988                                               | 24/7 in the US & Canada |
| 988 Online Chat               | [https://chat.988lifeline.org/](https://chat.988lifeline.org/) | Online Chat             |
| Crisis Text Line              | Text `HOME` to 741741                                          | 24/7 Text Support       |


### International
Professional help is available globally. You may be able to use the 988 Online Chat if you are outside of North America. For local, country-specific assistance:

- **Your Local Emergency Number:** Always know your country's local emergency number (e.g., `999`, `112`, `000`, etc.) for immediate risk situations.
- **Crisis Line Directory:** For a comprehensive list of suicide crisis lines by country, the *[List of Suicide Crisis Lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_suicide_crisis_lines)* Wikipedia article is a good place to start.
