# Support Requests

Our support ticket system, powered by our Discord bot Nixi, is the primary and most efficient way to get assistance from the community staff team. Please use this system for all private support needs, administrative questions, and detailed issue reports.

## Before Opening a Ticket
We highly recommend consulting this support website and FAQ first, as many common issues may already be resolved by referencing the content contained herein. However, if you are experiencing a unique problem or require private administrative assistance, please feel free to open a ticket.

## Opening a Ticket

1. Navigate to the [#support](https://discord.com/channels/555868544244514836/1277127604477890570) channel on the [community Discord server](https://discord.xeriscape.network/). 
2. Click the "Open A Ticket" button in the interaction provided by the Nixi within the channel. 
3. A new private thread will automatically be created within the #support channel, visible only to you and the staff team. 
4. In this new thread, clearly state the reason for your ticket. Include all necessary details, usernames, and evidence (screenshots/videos) to allow staff to assist you quickly.

=== "Discord Server/Bots"

    For general issues related to the Telzenith Discord server infrastructure or the Nixi bot, please provide details through a ticket:

    - **Roles & Permissions:** Report incorrect/missing roles or issues with access and permissions.
    - **Nixi Bot Issues:** Please provide any relevant details such as commands executed and message IDs for both the execution and the bot responses, if applicable.
    - **Administrative & Private Issues:** Use a ticket for reporting rule violations, appealing punishments, and requesting administrative assistance.
    - **Moderation Filter Issues:** If the issue is related to a moderation filter (e.g., incorrect word flagging), please report it directly to ZeNinja864 instead of opening a ticket.

=== "Minecraft Server (Telzenith)"

    Due to the multitude of plugins, scripts, and external tools running on the Minecraft server, bugs and errors can occasionally crop up. For members who come across these issues, you are requested to submit a support thread that includes all relevant information:

    - Description: A clear explanation of what happened.
    - Outcome: What the actual result was (e.g., "The server crashed," "I lost my items").
    - Intended Outcome: What should have happened (e.g., "The machine should have run smoothly").
    - Steps to Reproduce: A step-by-step guide on how the staff can replicate the issue.

    For issues related to crashes and disconnects, client logs may be requested. Client logs can be located within the .minecraft game directory, which is typically found at the following default paths:

    - Windows: `%APPDATA%\.minecraft`
    - MacOS: `~/Library/Application Support/minecraft`
    - Linux: `~/.minecraft`

    Minecraft client logs can be directly uploaded to the ticket thread if they are small enough. For larger files, or if you wish to redact information such as IP addresses for additional privacy, you can upload the logs instead either to [MCPaste](https://mcpaste.io/) or [MCLogs](https://mclo.gs/).

## Expectations & Guidelines

- **Be Patient:** Staff members are volunteers and may not be available 24/7. Please allow sufficient time (up to 24 hours) for a response before bumping your ticket.
- **Private Thread:** Tickets are private and limited to only you and the staff team. If you need to include another member in the discussion, please use the command: `/ticket add <USER>`.
- **Limit One Active Ticket:** To ensure clarity, efficient resolution, and proper queue management, community members are limited to opening one active ticket at a time. Please ensure your current ticket is resolved and closed before submitting a new request.
- **Avoid Spam/Abuse:** Do not open unnecessary or spam tickets. Repeated misuse of the ticket system will result in administrative action, including a ban from filing future tickets.
- **Be Respectful:** Maintain courteous and respectful communication with staff members.
- **Do Not Ping Staff:** Do not directly ping staff members (using `@username`) in a newly opened ticket. The bot automatically notifies the team members when a new ticket is created.

### Don't Ask to Ask
Please do not open tickets asking to ask for support; If you are encountering issues and need support, please simply open a ticket and provide all the relevant details, someone will help you when they are available to do so.

Also, please do not hijack, or post in, other channels of the Discord server to request support for your issue or for someone to review your ticket; If you are having an issue, please create your own ticket and someone will assist you when possible.

If you are in need of assistance when it comes to formulating a question, some resources that can provide such information are:

* [How do I ask a good question?](https://stackoverflow.com/help/how-to-ask)
* [Don't Ask to Ask, Just Ask](https://dontasktoask.com/)
* [No Hello](https://nohello.net/en/)
