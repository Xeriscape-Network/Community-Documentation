# Ranks
Our community is organized into several distinct ranks and roles to help manage our various gaming branches, reward our supporters, and provide a clear structure for all members. Below is an overview of how these roles are categorized and what they represent within the community.

## Staff & Leadership
These roles are responsible for the health, safety, and daily operation of our community.

- **Administrator:** The leadership team responsible for server infrastructure, high-level decision-making, and long-term community strategy.
    - Some Administrators may also hold the specialized title of **Administrative Operations** (often referred to as **"AO"** or **"Operations"**), which serves as a sub-rank for those managing specific technical, backend, or organizational tasks.
- **Moderator:** The primary staff rank for most community interactions. While not the only staff level, Moderators make up the majority of our team and are the first point of contact for members. They ensure that everyone follows the community guidelines and maintains a positive environment for all.

## Exclusive Designations
Unique roles awarded for specific contributions or history within the community.

- **Founder:** An exclusive rank reserved for our Private and OG Members. This role recognizes those who were here at the very beginning and helped build the foundation of our community.
- **Booster:** A special role for those who support the server directly via Discord Server Boosting. This role typically comes with its own set of visual perks and recognition.

## Game-Specific Access
We support a variety of different games, each with its own dedicated space. Exclusive to our Discord server, you must have the corresponding role to see the channels for these games:

### Minecraft
- **Minecrafter:** Grants access to the Minecraft-focused section of our Discord server, allowing you to coordinate and chat with other players in our community.

### Destiny 2
- **Guardian:** The base role for anyone interested in Destiny 2. This grants access to our Destiny-focused channels. Please note that to receive this role, you must have [your Bungie.net account linked to your Discord account](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8063233404823-Connections-Linked-Roles-Community-Members#:~:text=Data%20transparency-,Setting%20Up%20Your%20Connections%20%26%20Connection%20Details,allow%20you%20to%20add%2C%20remove%2C%20and%20manage%20all%20of%20your%20connections.,-Some%20connections%20have) and also have [your Discord account linked with the Charlemagne bot (Warmind.io)](https://warmind.io/auth/discord).
- **Destiny Clan Member:** This role is assigned automatically by the Charlemagne bot to any members who are currently in our in-game clan. As a prerequisite, you must already hold the **Guardian** rank to be eligible for this automated assignment.
