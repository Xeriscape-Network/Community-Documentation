# Welcome to Telzenith!

Welcome to Telzenith, the official Vanilla+ Minecraft Survival Server of The Xeriscape Network! We're thrilled to have you join our community.

Telzenith aims to provide an enhanced Vanilla experience, enriched with carefully selected plugins and scripts to make your gameplay even more enjoyable.

This welcome guide is your starting point, offering all the necessary information to connect to and set up your adventure on our server. We want to ensure you have a smooth and enjoyable experience from the very beginning.

To get started, please take a look at [our article on connecting to the server](./connecting.md). To unlock the full Telzenith experience, it's essential to [link your Minecraft UUID to your Discord ID](./linking.md). This allows us to ensure a secure and personalized environment. We've prepared a [handy guide](./linking.md) to walk you through the Discord account linking process.

If you ever encounter any issues, have questions, or simply need assistance, our friendly community is here to help. Feel free to ask in our general community channels, or if you need more focused support, please open a ticket in the [#support](https://discord.com/channels/555868544244514836/1277127604477890570) channel of our [Discord server](https://discord.xeriscape.network/). We're always happy to assist you!
