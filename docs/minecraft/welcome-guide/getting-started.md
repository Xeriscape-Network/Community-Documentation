# Getting Started
Welcome to the server! Once you’ve connected to Telzenith, you might be wondering what to do next. This guide will help you navigate your first few minutes and set you up for a successful journey.

## Spawn & Navigation
When you first join, you will arrive at the server Spawn. This is a safe zone where you can meet other players and access essential server information.

The "Wilderness" refers to any area on the server that isn't protected by a server-side region or another player's claim. To start your adventure, you'll need to venture out into these unprotected lands to find a spot to set up. You can walk out manually, or use the `/rtp` command to randomly teleport yourself thousands of blocks away from spawn to find a secluded area. Once you find a spot you like, use `/sethome [name]` so you can easily return there later with `/home [name]`.

## Protecting Your Home
While griefing is not allowed on Telzenith, the best way to stay safe is to use our claim system. You start with enough blocks for a small base. You can use a Golden Shovel to mark two opposite corners of your area to create a protection. If you're playing with a group, remember to `/trust` them so they can help you build within your boundaries. For a deep dive into land protection, refer to our [Player Protection Claims Guide](../features/claims).

## Resource Gathering
While you are free to gather resources anywhere in the Wilderness, we encourage players to be mindful of the landscape. Large-scale mining or massive deforestation should ideally be done away from shared community areas to keep the main world looking its best. Always remember that any structure or chest left in the Wilderness without a claim is unprotected and can be accessed and modified by any player.

If you happen across a generated structure (e.g. dungeon or temple) that appears to have been looted by others, feel free to check it out anyway. Loot in these chests is instanced, meaning every player gets their own unique rewards. These rewards will also regenerate over time, ensuring that exploration remains rewarding for everyone.

## Communication
Our chat is split into channels to keep things organized. By default, you'll be in Local Chat, meaning only players nearby can hear you. To talk to everyone (and Discord), use the `!` prefix before your message (e.g., `!Hello everyone!`).

If you prefer to switch your active channel entirely, you can use /chat to open a management menu, or use `/ch <channel>` (for example, `/ch global`) to switch focus. You can check the [Chat System](../features/chat) article for a full list of commands and specialized channels.

## Skills and Progression
As you perform everyday tasks like mining, woodcutting, or farming, you will level up your mcMMO skills. You can view your current stats at any time by typing `/mcstats`. Higher levels unlock "Super Abilities," such as Super Breaker for pickaxes, which you can activate by right-clicking while holding a tool and then hitting a block.

## Essential Commands

These are the primary commands you will use to navigate the world and interact with other players. Additional commands can be viewed via the `/help` command.

| **Command**               | **Function**                                                      |
|---------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `/spawn`                  | Returns you to the server spawn                                   |
| `/rtp`                    | Teleports you to a random, unprotected location in the wilderness |
| `/warps`                  | Opens a GUI menu listing all warp locations across the server     |
| `/sethome [name]`         | Sets your current location as a home, with an optional name       |
| `/home [name]`            | Teleports you back to your home                                   |
| `/tpa <player>`           | Sends a request to teleport to another player                     |
| `/tpahere <player>`       | Sends a request to a player to teleport to you                    |
| `/msg <player> <message>` | Sends a private message to another player                         |
| `/balance` or `/money`    | Displays your current bank account balance                        |
| `rules`                   | Displays the server rules in chat                                 |

## Getting Assistance
If you ever find yourself stuck or have a question that isn't covered in the wiki, our community and staff are here to help. You can ask for assistance from staff members directly if they are online in-game. For more complex issues, reporting players, or tracking a bug, please use our Discord Ticket System to ensure your request is documented and resolved by the team.

## Your Next Steps
Once you have established your first claim, we recommend exploring the rest of the information covered here in this website. Specifically, take a look at the remainder of the [Welcome Guide](./) and the [Server Features](../features) section to see everything the server has to offer.

If you would like to connect with our community even when you aren't in-game, be sure to join our [Discord](https://discord.xeriscape.network/). It is the best place to chat with players, get notified about server news, and stay up to date with the latest announcements.

While linking your Discord ID and Minecraft UUID/XUID not a requirement to play on the server, we highly recommend [linking your Discord ID with your Minecraft account](./linking). This brings several benefits, including syncing your in-game ranks and roles with the Discord server, and allowing you to interact with the Minecraft server from your phone or PC through our bridged chat!
