# Titles

Titles are a fun and customizable way to showcase your achievements, status, and history on the server. They appear as a separate line of customized text just below your in-game username on the nameplate above your character.

## Title Management
Titles are entirely optional. Players can earn as many as they like and choose to wear none at all if they prefer a clean look. They can earn and unlock a title without having to display it. Management is handled conveniently through the `/titles` menu, which allows players to easily view, equip, and unequip their earned titles at any time.

## Earning Titles
Titles are generally earned through reaching specific milestones or completing certain advancements on the server. These are some sources of titles:

- **Utilities:** Free titles are available that dynamically display real-time information, such as your current health, total McMMO power level, or current rank on the server.
- **Advancements:** Earned by completing advancements. For example, the *Dragonslayer* title is given to anyone who defeats the Ender Dragon and completes the *Free the End* advancement.
- **Skills & Milestones:** Awarded for reaching specific level thresholds in jobs or leveling up skills.

## The Flawless Title
The *Flawless* title is a special status title designed to honor players who have maintained a deathless run on the server. Every new player who joins the server starts with the *Flawless* title available to be equipped immediately. However, this title is unique in its loss conditions. If a player dies at any point after joining, access to the *Flawless* title is permanently revoked, and they are automatically granted the *Tarnished* title instead, which serves as a permanent record of their first death. This title is essentially a deathless challenge, reserved only for players who successfully maintain their perfect streak.

## Limited Availability Titles
Some titles are not permanently available to all players and are designated as special or rare. If you have acquired one of these titles, you will retain it, but the method of acquisition is restricted:

- **Legacy Titles:** Titles that were available during a specific phase of the server's history but are no longer acquirable by new or existing players.
- **Seasonal/Event Titles:** Titles that can only be earned or unlocked during specific times of the year, such as holiday events, seasonal competitions, or anniversary celebrations. They are generally unavailable outside of that window. 
- **Staff-Awarded Titles:** Titles that are manually given out by server staff to recognize exemplary community members, specific contest winners, or outstanding positive contributions to the server. These cannot be unlocked through automated systems.

Check the dedicated titles menu in-game (usually accessed via the `/titles`) to see the full list of available titles and their specific unlock requirements.
