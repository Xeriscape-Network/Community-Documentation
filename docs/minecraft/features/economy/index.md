# Economy

The Telzenith server operates on a robust, player-driven economy designed to encourage trade, collaboration, and community engagement. Our in-game currency is the dollar ($). While our systems provide the necessary tools and infrastructure, you, the player determine item prices, supply and demand, and the general direction of personal spending.

## Sources of Income
The primary way to earn dollars is through interacting with players and server features:

- **Diamonds:** A guaranteed source of income is selling Diamonds directly to the server shop for $10 each. This provides a steady baseline income that every player can rely on. 
- **Chest Shops:** Selling resources, mob drops, or bulk materials to other players.
- **Auction House:** List and selling high-demand items directly to other players.
- **Jobs:** These offer opportunities to earn a steady, baseline passive income simply by engaging with the game's mechanics and completing initial or daily tasks.

## Spending Opportunities
Dollars are best utilized to enhance your quality of life, expand your reach, and acquire items or services you cannot produce yourself:

- **Acquiring Goods:** Purchasing items from other player shops. 
- **Land Management:** Purchase additional claim blocks, protecting your builds and resources. 
- **Player Warps:** Create fast travel warps for private or public use.

Community Investment: Contributing to community projects, public farms, or bidding on unique server-run auctions.
