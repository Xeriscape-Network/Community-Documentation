# Troubleshooting

This section of the support knowledgebase is dedicated to offering solutions and guidance for fixing problems related to your interactions with the Telzenith server. Whether you are facing technical difficulties or security concerns, the resources below will guide you toward a resolution.

## Common Issues & Solutions
To quickly find help, identify the category that best matches the problem you are experiencing:

- **Connection Problems:** If you are having issues connecting to the server (e.g., timeouts, disconnections, high latency), refer to our [MTR Diagnostic Guide](./connection).
- **Game Performance:** If you are having issues with game performance (low frame rates, stuttering, client-side lag), check out our [Performance Optimization Guide](./performance) for settings adjustments and client-side tips. 
- **Account Security Concerns:** If you are having account security issues (e.g., suspicious activity, password concerns), check out our dedicated [Account Security Guide](./account-security) for tips regarding how to secure your account and steps to take if your account is compromised.

!!! info "Additional Support"

    If you cannot find a resolution to your issue within the guides above, please submit a ticket on our Discord support channel for direct staff assistance. For information on what details and diagnostic information to provide to speed up your support request, please refer to our dedicated [Support Ticket Guide].
