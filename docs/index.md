???+ tip "Work In Process Documentation"

    The Xeriscape Network is a work-in-progress community and is still in its early stages of development. As a result, some sections of the community, such as the Discord server and this documentation website, may be incomplete, temporary, or missing features. Additionally, the community documentation is currently being rewritten and updated, so some articles may be under construction or contain outdated information during this transition. If you would like to assist with this effort, we welcome you to join our [Discord server](https://discord.xeriscape.network/).

Welcome to The Xeriscape Network! Founded on March 23, 2010, The Xeriscape Network has existed as a community of friends brought together by their love of gaming; We welcome members from all walks of life and currently consist of an international membership.
